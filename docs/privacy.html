Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Vogel ("the app", "we") is provided by BUSATER YAZILIM ANONIM SIRKETI. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app, where it is stored and how it is used.

2. Account Information

You can start using the app without an account. To save your progress and carry it between devices, you need to create one. When you create an account we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name (if provided)
  • Your sign-in method (email, Google or Apple)

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only see the relay address Apple generates; we cannot access your real email address. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

3. Your Learning Progress

The following data about your learning is kept:

  • XP, level and completed lessons
  • Daily streak and heart count
  • Words you have seen and your exam results
  • Theme, language and notification preferences

If you have an account, this data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore) and linked to your account so you do not lose progress when you change devices. Without an account, it stays only on your device.

4. Device and Usage Information

To understand which countries and devices the app is used on, we record:

  • Country and language — read from your device region setting
  • Device brand, model and type (phone/tablet)
  • Operating system and app version
  • When you last opened the app
  • Your subscription status

This information is used only to produce aggregate statistics and is not sold to third parties. We do not request location permission, we do not infer location from your IP address, and we do not use your advertising identifier for this purpose.

5. Microphone

The microphone is accessed only during speaking exercises, when you start a recording. Your recording is used for pronunciation assessment and is not stored on our servers.

6. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. We never have access to your card or payment details; these are handled solely by Apple and Google.

We use RevenueCat to track your subscription status. Your account identifier, email address and purchase status are shared with RevenueCat.

7. Advertising

Free users are shown ads through Google AdMob. Premium subscribers see no ads.

On iOS you are asked for permission for personalised advertising (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not targeted to you, and the app continues to work with all its features. Your in-app learning data is not used for ad targeting.

8. Error Reporting

Technical error reports are collected via Sentry to improve app stability. These reports contain the error message, device model and app version; they do not contain your learning content or password.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete your account and all cloud data from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it; you must cancel it separately through the App Store or Google Play. See also our account deletion page.

10. Third-Party Services

Some of your data is processed through these services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and progress sync
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — subscription status tracking
  • Sentry — error reporting
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — authentication

Each service is subject to its own privacy policy.

11. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for users aged 4 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

13. Contact

For questions about our privacy policy: bsdigitalapp@gmail.com
